# Kollektiv

**A professional community platform built around skills, ideas, projects, collaboration, and career opportunities.**

Kollektiv helps people turn professional participation into meaningful career opportunities by connecting them with relevant communities, people, projects, discussions, and opportunities.

> **Vision:** Turn professional participation into career opportunities.

---

##  What is Kollektiv?

Kollektiv is designed for students, professionals, creators, employers, and people developing skills in different fields.

Instead of focusing primarily on followers and popularity, Kollektiv focuses on **professional participation, useful contributions, skills, projects, and opportunities**.

Users can join professional fields, participate in discussions, showcase their work, connect with others, and discover relevant jobs and internships.

---

##  Core Features

### Professional Profiles
Create a professional profile showcasing:

- Skills
- Projects
- Experience
- Career goals
- Professional interests
- Contributions
- Achievements

###  Professional Fields
Join fields based on your professional interests and discover relevant:

- Discussions
- People
- Guides
- Skills
- Projects
- Opportunities

###  Discussions & Ideas
Share ideas, ask questions, participate in discussions, and collaborate with people in your field.

###  Project Showcase
Showcase projects and demonstrate practical skills through real work and contributions.

###  Collaboration
Connect with people who share similar professional interests and discover potential collaborators.

###  Jobs & Internships
Discover relevant career opportunities based on your field, skills, and professional goals.

###  Employer Profiles
Organizations can build professional profiles and connect with potential candidates.

### Skills & Reputation
Build professional credibility through demonstrated skills, projects, contributions, and recognition.

###  Secure Messaging
Communicate directly with other members through secure professional messaging.

###  Reporting & Moderation
Kollektiv includes reporting and moderation features designed to maintain a professional and trustworthy community.

## MVP

The initial version of Kollektiv focuses on the core experience:

1. **User registration and authentication**
2. **Professional profiles**
3. **Joining professional fields**
4. **Creating and discussing posts**
5. **Showcasing projects**
6. **Connecting and collaborating**
7. **Discovering relevant jobs and internships**

Additional features will be introduced as the platform evolves.
